AI BUILDER CANDIDATE ASSIGNMENT BRIEF
KPMG — Assessment exercise for AI Builder Senior Consultant and Manager candidates
================================================================================

Timebox: Maximum 3 hours total
Tools:   Candidate's own tools, accounts, and AI tokens

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ASSESSMENT-ONLY ASSURANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This exercise is intended to evaluate your approach, judgment, and builder mindset.
It is not a request to produce a formal hiring asset for immediate or current-cycle
use. Nothing submitted as an assignment will be used by KPMG for anything other than
the candidate's assessment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ASSIGNMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KPMG is hiring for an AI Builder role. The role is intentionally broad: strong
candidates may come from software engineering, UX, MLOps, consulting, research,
operations, product, or self-taught paths. What matters is the ability to enter an
ambiguous enterprise context, identify where AI can create practical value, build
something tangible, and explain tradeoffs clearly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TASK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Build something you think would evaluate an AI Builder well. We are giving you a
starting point, not a fixed problem: help the hiring team identify strong AI Builder
candidates, using the AI Builder job description as your source material. Part of the
task is deciding what "evaluate well" actually means, then building to your own
definition. You choose the direction and the solution format. If you reframe the
problem, tell us why.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE FORMAT IS OPEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AI Builders join us from engineering, UX, MLOps, consulting, research, operations,
product, and self-taught paths. A single fixed task would advantage one background
and penalize the rest, and would tell us more about prior training than about how a
candidate thinks. Leaving the format open lets you lean into your strengths and show
your strongest work in your own domain. We want to see what you are actually good at,
not watch you perform a skill you do not have. What every strong background shares is
clear reasoning under ambiguity and constraint, and that is what we are looking for
across all of them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE'RE REALLY EVALUATING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We are not hiring you to build hiring tools. This exercise uses "evaluate an AI
Builder well" as a deliberately open canvas, because the AI Builder role itself is
open. You will routinely walk into ambiguous problems with no defined solution and
decide what is worth building, and often what the real problem even is. The topic is
the vehicle. What we assess is how you get from a vague prompt to a concrete,
defensible build: how you scope the problem, what you choose to optimize for, the
choices you make, and the ones you deliberately do not.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU CAN BUILD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- A lightweight product, app, workflow, agent, or automation.
- An evaluation process, scoring or interview tool, or simulation.
- A candidate workbench, reviewer workflow, prompt/evaluation framework, or prototype
  review process.
- Another practical artifact that helps evaluate AI Builder candidates.

There is no single correct answer. Choose the approach that best shows how you think
and build.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED SUBMISSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Artifact:
  A working prototype, workflow, process, tool, demo, rubric, simulation, or clearly
  described system that evaluates AI Builder candidates.

3-minute video:
  - First 1 minute: Show what you built.
  - Remaining 2 minutes: Explain your thinking process, how you framed the problem,
    key choices, tradeoffs, risks, AI use, and what you would improve with more time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULES AND GUARDRAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Spend no more than 3 hours total, including building, preparing your explanation,
  and recording the video. No extra credit for more time. The cap is tight on purpose.
  We want to see what you choose to leave out.

- Use your own tools, accounts, and AI tokens. Free-tier tools are completely fine.
  We are assessing judgment, not spend. KPMG will not provide tools, tokens,
  credentials, data, or environments for this exercise.

- Use only synthetic, public, or sample data. Do not use confidential or personal data.

- Make your artifact inspectable with the lightest supporting materials needed:
  demo link, local files, screenshots, a README, sample outputs, or clear instructions.

- We expect you to use AI tools. This role does not exist without them. But remember:
  you can outsource thinking, you cannot outsource understanding. We will ask follow-up
  questions, and you will be expected to answer them. You should be able to disclose
  how you used AI and which decisions were yours.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE WILL LOOK FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Understanding of the AI Builder role and the hiring challenge.
- Ability to turn ambiguity into a concrete direction, including how you frame the problem.
- A practical, usable artifact grounded in the job context.
- Evidence of builder mindset: making something real, not only describing an idea.
- Communication of assumptions, tradeoffs, risks, and next steps.
- Responsible AI judgment, including fairness, governance, transparency, and candidate
  experience.

We are not looking for polish for its own sake. We are looking for evidence that you
can think in systems, build pragmatically, communicate clearly, and balance speed with
judgment in a complex enterprise environment.

"Like many complex organizations, KPMG has competing priorities, a need for risk,
security and appropriate governance and a desire to move faster and be innovative in
the way we operate and deliver to clients - you are expected to consider the needs and
sometimes competing priorities of multiple stakeholders when considering how you
approach answering this question."

APPENDIX A: AI BUILDER JOB DESCRIPTION — SENIOR CONSULTANT
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------

At KPMG in Canada, our people bring their unique perspectives to Canada's most
important challenges. Here, you can build momentum that reaches beyond our business,
develop skills for the future, and take ownership of your career with support at every
stage.

KPMG in Canada is scaling an internal team of AI Builders who operate as the engine
embedded in the business — powering the firm's agent builds and transforming our
business.

This team is for people who sit at the intersection of enterprise operations,
technology, and design, and who can look at how the firm works today, imagine how it
should operate with agentic AI, and make that future real through execution.

Objective: Move beyond experimentation and enable real internal adoption of agentic AI,
redesigning core workflows, decision systems, and operating models using autonomous
agents that power KPMG's service lines and internal teams.

AI Builders take ownership of the full build lifecycle, working closely with Engineering
and Internal Transformation teams to move solutions from the KPMG AI Lab into
scalable, reliable production systems, within a complex and regulated enterprise.

WHAT YOU WILL DO (Senior Consultant)
-------------------------------------

At the Senior Consultant level, you will be a hands-on AI Builder who takes ownership
of well-scoped internal problems and contributes to building real, agentic solutions
end to end. You will work closely with senior builders, practice leads, and internal
stakeholders on rapid experimentation, prototyping, and Lab-based initiatives.

In this role, you will:
- Build, test, and harden agents, plugins, skills, and tools using the firm's approved
  technology stack, working with cloud and engineering teams to deliver
  production-grade solutions.
- Own the full build lifecycle: prototype in the AI Lab, productionize through Internal
  Transformation, and maintain solutions post-deployment.
- Work with production systems, design evaluations, and handle integrations with
  enterprise platforms and data sources.
- Contribute reusable skills, components, and tools back into the shared AI stack so
  each subsequent build starts further ahead.
- Operate inside the shared builder pool, deployed against the highest-leverage agent
  builds across the organization.
- Be an active member of the AI Lab, contributing demos, reusable assets, and helping
  build a strong builder community across the firm.

WHAT YOU BRING (Senior Consultant)
------------------------------------

- Enterprise fluency: understand how organizations operate.
- Identify real pain points, constraints, and opportunities where agentic AI creates
  tangible value.
- Think in workflows, not just models — multi-step processes, human-AI handoffs,
  decision boundaries, appropriate autonomy levels.
- Builder mindset: create solutions, not just describe them (code, config, low-code,
  APIs, orchestration tools).
- End-to-end ownership: problem definition through to working system.
- Comfortable in ambiguity; enterprise transformation is rarely linear.
- Make reasonable assumptions, build quickly, test, learn, iterate.
- Sound judgment on risk, governance, ethics, and trust — core design constraints,
  not afterthoughts.

WHERE YOU'LL WORK
-----------------
KPMG AI Labs in Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, or Montreal.
Embedded in business units: building and testing agentic workflows and prototypes.

WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU (Senior Consultant)
-----------------------------------------
- Work on genuinely new agentic AI use cases at the experimentation and prototyping stage.
- Exposure to complex, regulated, enterprise-scale internal problems and workflows.
- Hands-on experience building demos, prototypes, and showcase artifacts.
- Multidisciplinary environment: builders, researchers, designers, business professionals.
- AI Lab with secure infrastructure, frontier models, and applied research.
- Access to frontier models and platforms, including early exposure to leading foundation
  models, agent frameworks, and enterprise-grade AI tooling.

APPENDIX B: AI BUILDER JOB DESCRIPTION — MANAGER
================================================================================

WHAT YOU WILL DO (Manager)
----------------------------

At the Manager level, you will lead delivery of internal agentic AI initiatives, taking
ownership of complex problems and guiding work from experimentation through validated
outcomes. High degree of autonomy; works with Senior Managers, service line leaders,
and AI Builders.

In this role, you will:
- Lead design, build, and deployment of production-grade agents, plugins, skills, and
  tools using the firm's approved AI and engineering stack.
- Own the full build lifecycle: Lab prototyping, evaluations, productionization,
  change management, and ongoing product ownership.
- Partner with cloud platform and engineering teams for code quality, reliability,
  security, and scalability.
- Ensure demo readiness: narrative, artifacts, appropriate demo safety and governance.
- Package validated prototypes into reusable demos, kits, and handoff materials.
- Balance speed with judgment; embed risk, governance, and trust considerations.
- Provide delivery and technical leadership to Consultants and Senior Consultants.
- Capture and share learnings through one-pagers, replication kits, and internal AI
  literacy artifacts.

WHAT YOU BRING (Manager)
--------------------------

- Strong enterprise fluency: large organizations, cross-functional, strategy + execution.
- Identify high-impact opportunities for agentic AI; guide teams on prioritizing use cases.
- Think in systems and workflows; oversee human-AI collaboration and governance.
- Lead with builder mindset: concept to execution, tangible + production-ready solutions.
- End-to-end ownership at scale: problem shaping through deployment and adoption.
- Comfortable in ambiguity; lead teams through evolving problem spaces.
- Build, test, iterate with intention; make informed tradeoffs; coach others.
- Embed responsible AI by design: risk, governance, ethics, trust are core constraints.
- Lead and develop teams; create environment for builders to do their best work.

WHERE YOU'LL WORK
-----------------
KPMG AI Labs in Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, or Montreal.
Embedded in business units: building and testing agentic workflows and prototypes.

WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU (Manager)
--------------------------------
- Lead and shape how agentic AI is explored, demonstrated, and validated inside a large
  complex professional services firm.
- Ownership of high-impact experimentation and prototyping that influences internal
  transformation and future delivery models.
- Multidisciplinary environment: builders, researchers, designers, client service teams,
  business leaders.
- AI Lab with secure infrastructure, frontier models, harnesses, and applied research.
- Develop leadership experience: guide teams, set direction, define how agentic AI
  capabilities move from concept to something real.
